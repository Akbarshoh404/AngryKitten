Privacy Policy for Spinject

**Last updated:** April 8, 2026

## Introduction

Spinject respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile game collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app.

By using Spinject, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## Information We Collect

Spinject is designed to be simple and privacy-friendly. We do **not collect personal data from users externally**.

However, the app may store the following information **locally on your device**:

- Player name (entered by the user)
- Game progress (scores, waves, statistics)
- Selected game settings (such as shot patterns)

This data is stored using Android’s **SharedPreferences** and **never leaves your device**.

---

## How We Use Information

The locally stored data is used only to:

- Save your game progress
- Maintain your preferences
- Improve your gameplay experience

We do **not** use your data for analytics, tracking, or advertising.

---

## Data Sharing

We do **not**:

- Sell your data
- Share your data with third parties
- Transfer your data outside your device

All information remains strictly on your device.

---

## Internet Usage

Spinject does **not require an internet connection** and does not transmit any user data over the internet.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Spinject is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

---

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

- You have full control over your data
- Uninstalling the app will delete all stored data

---

## Third-Party Services

Spinject currently does **not use any third-party services**, such as:

- Analytics tools
- Advertising networks
- External SDKs

If this changes in future updates, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

---

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
