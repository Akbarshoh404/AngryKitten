📱 Full Description (Play Market)

**CatLoop 🐱 – Addictive Arcade Challenge**

CatLoop is a fast-paced, cat-themed arcade game where your reflexes and timing are everything. Guide a bouncing cat around a rotating ring, avoid deadly spikes, and push your high score to the limit.

🎮 **Unique Gameplay**

Instead of controlling the character directly, you rotate the ring to influence the cat’s movement. It’s simple to learn but hard to master.

⚡ **Dynamic & Challenging**

- Increasing speed as you progress
- Random spike generation every bounce
- Up to 12 obstacles at higher levels
- Endless gameplay with rising difficulty

✨ **Features**

- Smooth physics-based movement
- Clean and modern UI with animations
- Fun sound effects and background music
- High score tracking
- Settings (music, vibration, stats)
- Lightweight and optimized for performance

🏆 **Challenge Yourself**

How long can you survive? Improve your reflexes and beat your best score every time you play.

Perfect for quick gaming sessions anytime, anywhere.

🔒 Privacy Policy

**Privacy Policy for CatLoop**

Effective Date: [Add Date]

CatLoop (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data.

## 1. Information We Collect

CatLoop does **not collect personal data** such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location

The app may store:

- Game progress (e.g., high scores)
- Settings (music, vibration preferences)

This data is stored **locally on your device only** using secure Android storage (DataStore).

## 2. How We Use Information

Stored data is used only to:

- Save your game progress
- Improve user experience
- Maintain app functionality

## 3. Third-Party Services

CatLoop currently does **not use third-party services** that collect personal data.
