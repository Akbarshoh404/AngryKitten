📜 Privacy Policy for ShiftBall

**Last updated:** April 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to ShiftBall. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

ShiftBall is a minimalistic offline endless runner game designed for entertainment purposes.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

## ✅ We do NOT collect personal data

ShiftBall:

- Does **not** collect personal information
- Does **not** require account registration
- Does **not** track your identity

---

## 📱 Local data stored on your device

The app stores the following data **locally only** using Android DataStore:

- Best score
- Game settings (theme, difficulty, sound, etc.)
- Preferences (e.g., vibration, music)

👉 This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not shared with us or third parties

---

## 3. Internet & Permissions

ShiftBall:

- Does **not require internet access**
- Does **not communicate with external servers**

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

ShiftBall currently:

- ❌ Does NOT use analytics services
- ❌ Does NOT use ads (e.g., AdMob)
- ❌ Does NOT use third-party SDKs that collect data

If this changes in future updates, this policy will be updated accordingly.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

ShiftBall is safe for all ages and:

- Does not knowingly collect data from children
- Does not include social features or tracking

---

## 6. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

- You have full control over your data
- Uninstalling the app removes all stored data

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future.

Any changes will be reflected with an updated “Last updated” date.
