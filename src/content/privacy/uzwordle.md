_Last updated: May 15, 2026_

Thank you for using **UzWordle** (“the App”).

UzWordle is designed with simplicity and privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

---

1. Information We Collect

UzWordle does **not** collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app does not require:

- account creation
- login/signup
- email address
- phone number
- location access
- contacts access

UzWordle is designed to work completely offline.

---

2. Local Data Storage

The app stores certain gameplay-related data locally on your device using Android DataStore.

This may include:

- game statistics
- win/loss counts
- streaks
- best Time Attack score
- settings/preferences
- theme preferences

This information:

- never leaves your device
- is not uploaded to any server
- is not shared with third parties

If you uninstall the app, this locally stored data may be deleted by Android.

---

3. Internet Access

UzWordle does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

The app does not:

- download user data
- upload gameplay data
- track user activity online

Future versions of the app may optionally introduce online features such as leaderboards or cloud sync. If that happens, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

---

4. Third-Party Services

UzWordle currently does not use:

- analytics services
- advertising SDKs
- tracking tools
- third-party authentication services

No personal data is shared with third parties.

---

5. Children’s Privacy

UzWordle is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

---

6. Data Security

Because UzWordle operates primarily offline and stores data locally on your device, your gameplay information remains under your control.

However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future as the app evolves.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

---

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the project repository or official app page.
