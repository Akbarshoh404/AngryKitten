## Privacy Policy for MathRow

**Effective Date:** May, 2026

MathRow respects your privacy.

---

## 📌 Data Collection

MathRow does **not collect, store, or share any personal data**.

We do not require:

- Account creation
- Email address
- Location data
- Contacts or media access

---

## 💾 Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data **only on your device** using Android DataStore:

- Game progress (levels reached)
- Settings (dark mode, language preference)
- High scores

This data never leaves your device.

---

## 🌐 Internet Access

MathRow works completely **offline** and does not use the internet for:

- Tracking
- Analytics
- Advertising
- Data syncing

---

## 👶 Children’s Privacy

The app is safe for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any data from children or any users.

---

## 📦 Third-Party Services

MathRow does not use any third-party analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs.

---

## ✉️ Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the Play Store listing.
