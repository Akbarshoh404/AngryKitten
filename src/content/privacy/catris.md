Privacy Policy for Catris

Last Updated: May 2026

Catris ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

## Information We Collect

Catris does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information.

The App does not require account creation and does not request personal information such as:

• Name

• Email address

• Phone number

• Physical address

• Payment information

## Local Data Storage

To provide gameplay features, Catris stores certain information locally on your device using Android DataStore.

This may include:

• Game settings (sound, vibration, theme preferences, ghost piece settings)

• Achievement progress

• Gameplay statistics

• High scores

• Other gameplay-related preferences

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Internet Access

Catris is designed to function offline and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

## Analytics and Tracking

Catris does not use analytics services to track users.

The App does not collect:

• Device identifiers for tracking

• Advertising identifiers

• Usage analytics

• Location information

## Advertising

Catris does not display third-party advertisements.

## Permissions

Catris may request the following permission:

• VIBRATE — Used exclusively to provide optional haptic feedback during gameplay.

This permission is not used to collect personal information.

## Children's Privacy

Catris is suitable for users of all ages. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## Data Security

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers, users maintain control over their stored game information.

## Third-Party Services

Catris does not integrate third-party services that collect personal information from users.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

## Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
