## Privacy Policy for Pulchi (Flo)

**Effective Date:** May 24, 2026

Pulchi ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data.

## Information We Collect

Pulchi is designed to operate primarily offline.

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

- Transactions and financial records
- Income and expense data
- Wallet information
- Budget limits
- Savings goals
- Debt records
- Categories and tags
- User preferences and settings

## Local Storage

All financial information entered into the App is stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database where supported by the Android operating system.

Pulchi does not require account creation and does not maintain user accounts on external servers.

## Data Sharing

Pulchi does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal financial information with third parties.

Financial data entered into the App remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

## Data Export

The App allows users to export financial information as CSV files. Exported files are created only when initiated by the user and are stored or shared according to the user's actions.

## Analytics and Advertising

Pulchi does not display third-party advertisements.

Pulchi does not use advertising identifiers for marketing purposes.

Pulchi does not collect personal financial data for analytics, profiling, or targeted advertising.

## Notifications

The App may generate local notifications on the device for reminders, recurring transactions, and budget alerts. These notifications are processed locally and do not require transmission of financial information to external servers.

## Security

Pulchi may provide optional biometric authentication using Android biometric APIs. Biometric information is processed by the Android operating system and is never collected, stored, or transmitted by the App.

## Children's Privacy

Pulchi is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## User Control

Users may:

- Modify their financial records
- Delete individual entries
- Export their data
- Clear all application data
- Uninstall the application at any time

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Updated versions will be made available through the application's store listing or official website.
