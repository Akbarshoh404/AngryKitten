Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy for Angry Kitten Sudoku

Effective Date: May 13, 2026

Angry Kitten Sudoku respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information Collection

Angry Kitten Sudoku does not collect, store, or share personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Location data
- Contacts
- Photos or files

The app works primarily offline and stores gameplay-related data locally on your device.

## Local Storage

The app uses local device storage (shared_preferences) to save:

- Game statistics
- Achievement progress
- Theme settings
- Language preferences
- Gameplay settings

This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

## Internet Access

The app may use internet access only for:

- Google Play services
- App updates
- Crash reporting or analytics provided by platform services (if enabled in future versions)

Currently, Angry Kitten Sudoku does not operate its own servers for user accounts or cloud storage.

These services may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## Children’s Privacy

Angry Kitten Sudoku is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data Security

Because most app data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for maintaining access to your device securely.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions of the app. Any updates will be reflected within the app listing or application materials.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the Google Play listing contact information.
