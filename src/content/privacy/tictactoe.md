This Privacy Policy explains how the **Tic Tac Toe** mobile application (“the App”) handles user information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Our app is designed for entertainment and **does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data**.

## **2.1 Personal Data**

We **do not collect** any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

## **2.2 Device Data**

We **do not collect** any device identifiers, device information, or technical data.

## **2.3 Usage Data**

We **do not collect** analytics, gameplay statistics, or activity logs.

## **2.4 Children’s Data**

The app may be used by people of all ages, including children under 13.

We **do not collect any data** from children or adults.

Since **no data is collected**, no information is used or processed.
