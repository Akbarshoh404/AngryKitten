**Privacy Policy for Nerva**

Last updated: May 2026

Nerva ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

## Information We Collect

## Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally:

- Username
- Personal best scores
- XP and level progress
- Daily streak information
- Achievement progress
- App preferences and settings
- Theme preferences
- Gameplay statistics

This information remains on your device and is used to provide app functionality.

## Information Collected When You Sign In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we may collect:

- Google account identifier
- Display name
- Profile photo URL (if provided by Google)

This information is used solely for account identification and leaderboard functionality.

## Leaderboard Data

When signed in, the App may upload:

- Username
- Benchmark scores
- Ranking information
- User identifier required for leaderboard management

Leaderboard information may be visible to other users.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide and maintain app functionality
- Save progress and achievements
- Display leaderboards and rankings
- Improve app performance and reliability
- Analyze usage trends through analytics
- Prevent abuse of online services

## Analytics

Nerva may use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage information, including:

- App interactions
- Device information
- Session duration
- Performance and crash data

This information helps improve the application experience.

## Third-Party Services

Nerva may use the following third-party services:

- Google Sign-In
- Firebase Authentication
- Firebase Realtime Database
- Firebase Analytics

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

## Data Storage

Most user data is stored locally on your device. Cloud data is only stored when online features such as Google Sign-In and leaderboards are used.

## Children's Privacy

Nerva does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law without appropriate consent. If you believe personal information has been provided improperly, please contact us so it can be removed.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored and transmitted through the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including access, correction, or deletion of data associated with your account.

You may stop using online services at any time by signing out of your account.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date above.
