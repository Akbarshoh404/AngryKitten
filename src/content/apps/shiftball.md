Game is a fast-paced, minimalistic endless runner where precision and timing are everything.

Control a glowing ball as it races through an ever-changing corridor of obstacles. Tap anywhere to instantly shift sides, dodge incoming walls, and collect gems to build powerful combo multipliers. The longer you survive, the faster the game becomes.

Simple to play, challenging to master.
