Nerva – Train Your Brain Every Day

Nerva is a modern brain-training app designed to help you improve reaction speed, memory, focus, attention, and typing skills through fast benchmark-style challenges. Complete tests, earn XP, unlock achievements, build streaks, and compare your performance with players around the world.

Whether you want to sharpen your mind, challenge yourself daily, or simply track cognitive performance over time, Nerva provides a clean and engaging experience built for consistent practice.

Brain Training Tests

• Reaction Time
Measure how quickly you respond to visual stimuli.

• Sequence Memory
Remember and reproduce increasingly complex sequences.

• Aim Trainer
Improve speed and precision by hitting targets as quickly as possible.

• Number Memory
Challenge your ability to remember longer numbers over time.

• Verbal Memory
Test word recognition and retention skills.

• Color Match
Train focus and cognitive flexibility with color-based challenges.

• Typing Speed
Measure typing speed and accuracy with real-time performance tracking.

Progress & Achievements

• Gain XP after completing tests
• Level up your profile
• Maintain daily streaks
• Unlock achievements and milestones
• Track personal records for every benchmark
• Advance through rank tiers from Bronze Mind to Master Synapse

Leaderboards

Compete with players worldwide through optional online leaderboards. Sign in with Google to submit your best scores and see how you rank against other users.

Personal Statistics

Monitor your improvement over time with detailed progress tracking:

• Best scores for every test
• Total games played
• XP and level progression
• Achievement completion
• Daily streak history

Designed for Daily Practice

• Fast and repeatable training sessions
• Smooth and modern interface
• Light theme, dark theme, and system theme support
• Offline functionality for local training
• Optional cloud synchronization and leaderboards

Train consistently, challenge yourself, and discover how far your mind can improve with Nerva.
