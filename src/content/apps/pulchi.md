💰 Pulchi (Flo) — Private Personal Budget Tracker

Take control of your finances with Pulchi (Flo), a modern offline-first budget tracker designed for people who value privacy, speed, and simplicity.

No accounts. No subscriptions. No cloud databases. No data collection.

Everything stays securely on your device while giving you powerful tools to manage expenses, income, budgets, savings goals, debts, and multiple wallets.

📊 Track Every Transaction

Quickly record expenses and income with a clean and intuitive interface.

Features include:

• Expense tracking
• Income tracking
• Transaction categories
• Notes and descriptions
• Multiple wallets and accounts
• Transaction history
• Custom categories and tags

💳 Manage Multiple Wallets

Keep personal finances organized with separate wallets for:

• Cash
• Bank accounts
• Savings accounts
• Business funds
• Custom wallets

Monitor balances in real time and understand exactly where your money is stored.

🎯 Create Budgets & Stay on Track

Set monthly spending limits for categories and monitor progress throughout the month.

Pulchi automatically highlights:

• Budget usage percentage
• Approaching limits
• Overspending warnings
• Monthly spending trends

📈 Powerful Analytics

Understand your financial habits with visual reports and statistics.

Analyze:

• Spending by category
• Income vs expenses
• Monthly trends
• Budget performance
• Expense distribution
• Historical financial activity

Interactive charts help you identify patterns and make smarter financial decisions.

💰 Savings Goals

Create personalized savings goals and track your progress.

Examples:

• New phone
• Vacation fund
• Emergency savings
• Car purchase
• Education fund

Monitor contributions and stay motivated as you move toward your targets.

📅 Recurring Transactions

Automate routine financial records:

• Salary payments
• Rent expenses
• Utility bills
• Subscriptions
• Insurance payments

Recurring transactions are generated automatically according to your selected schedule.

📝 Debt Management

Track money you owe or money others owe you.

Features include:

• Debt records
• Remaining balance tracking
• Due dates
• Progress monitoring

🎨 Personalize Your Experience

Customize the app with:

• Dark mode
• Light mode
• System theme support
• Multiple currency symbols
• Adjustable budget settings
• Custom categories and colors

📤 Export Your Data

Maintain complete ownership of your information.

Export transaction records as CSV files for:

• Backups
• Personal archives
• Spreadsheet analysis
• Financial reporting

🔒 Privacy First

Your financial data belongs to you.

Pulchi is designed with privacy as a core principle:

• No account required
• No cloud synchronization
• No personal financial data sent to servers
• Local database storage only
• Optional biometric protection
• Full control over your information

⚡ Built for Modern Android

Built with:

• Kotlin
• Jetpack Compose
• Room Database
• Material 3 Design
• WorkManager
• Android Biometric Security

Fast, smooth, and optimized for modern Android devices.

Take control of your finances with Pulchi and build better money habits—completely offline and entirely private.
