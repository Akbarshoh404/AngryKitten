🟩🟨⬛ Welcome to UzWordle — a polished Wordle game in the Uzbek language!
Guess the hidden 5-letter Uzbek word and enjoy a clean, modern word puzzle built for Uzbek speakers and learners alike.
🎮 CLASSIC MODE
Guess the hidden 5-letter Uzbek word in 6 attempts. Get color-coded feedback after every guess:
🟩 Green — correct letter, correct spot
🟨 Yellow — correct letter, wrong spot
⬛ Gray — letter not in the word
The game includes a full Uzbek Latin keyboard with proper support for O', G', Sh, Ch, and Ng.
⚡ TIME ATTACK MODE
Race against a 60-second timer and score points for each word you solve. Build combos with consecutive correct answers to boost your score multiplier, then try to beat your personal best!
📊 STATISTICS & PROGRESS
Track your wins, losses, and streaks across sessions. View your guess distribution in a clear visual chart, with separate stats kept for Classic and Time Attack modes. All your data is saved locally on your device.
🎨 THOUGHTFUL DESIGN

Clean, modern Material 3 interface with dynamic theming
Smooth micro-animations on tiles, keyboard, and navigation
Dark mode support
Haptic feedback for key presses, wins, and errors

🇺🇿 BUILT FOR THE UZBEK LANGUAGE
UzWordle properly handles the Uzbek Latin alphabet, including multi-character letters like O', G', Sh, Ch, and Ng, each available as a single key on the keyboard. All apostrophe variants are normalized automatically so your guesses always match correctly.
📴 FULLY OFFLINE
No internet connection required. No ads interrupting your game. Just open the app and play anytime, anywhere.
Whether you want a relaxed daily word challenge or a fast-paced race against the clock, UzWordle offers a fun and engaging way to play with Uzbek words.
Download UzWordle and start guessing today! 😼
