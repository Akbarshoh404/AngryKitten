Full Description

Uybek is a modern real estate app for Uzbekistan that helps users buy, sell, and discover properties easily.

Browse apartments, houses, and other listings with search and filter tools, save favorites, chat directly with sellers, and publish your own property listings in minutes.

Features
Property search and filters
Detailed listing pages
Favorites and saved listings
In-app chat with sellers
Create and manage listings
Secure login with email, phone, or Google

Uybek is designed to provide a fast, simple, and convenient real estate experience for the Uzbekistan market.
