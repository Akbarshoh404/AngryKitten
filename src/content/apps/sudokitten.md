Angry Kitten Sudoku

A modern Sudoku experience built for clean gameplay, smooth mobile performance, and relaxing puzzle solving.

Angry Kitten Sudoku combines classic 9x9 Sudoku with polished visuals, responsive controls, achievements, statistics tracking, dark mode, and multilingual support — all in a lightweight offline app.

Whether you are new to Sudoku or already experienced, the game is designed to stay simple, readable, and enjoyable without unnecessary clutter.
