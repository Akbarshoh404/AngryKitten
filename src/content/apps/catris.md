🎮 Catris — Neon Retro Block Puzzle

Catris is a modern neon-styled block puzzle game inspired by classic arcade Tetris gameplay, rebuilt with smooth performance and immersive design using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

Step into a glowing retro arcade experience filled with dynamic animations, responsive controls, synthesized sound effects, and satisfying haptic feedback.

🌟 Key Features

✨ Neon Retro Design

Stylish 80s-inspired arcade UI
Dark & light theme support
Glassmorphism panels and glowing effects

🎮 Classic Block Puzzle Gameplay

Rotate, move, and drop tetromino blocks
Clear lines and score points
Increasing difficulty with faster levels

🔊 Synthesized Audio Engine

Real-time generated sound effects (no heavy audio files)
Unique tones for moves, clears, drops, and game over
Retro arcade-inspired sound design

📳 Haptic Feedback System

Lightweight vibration for actions
Strong feedback for hard drops and line clears
Immersive tactile gameplay experience

🏆 Achievements & Stats

Unlock 8+ achievements
Track total lines, score, play time, and more
Persistent progress saved locally

💾 Offline & Lightweight

Fully playable offline
No login required
No ads, no tracking

⚙️ Smooth Performance

Built with Jetpack Compose
Optimized game loop and rendering
Safe-area adaptive UI for all devices
🎯 How to Play
Move blocks left and right
Rotate to fit shapes perfectly
Drop blocks to complete full rows
Clear lines to score points
Reach higher levels as speed increases
Don’t let the stack reach the top!
📊 Scoring System
1 line: 40 × Level
2 lines: 100 × Level
3 lines: 300 × Level
4 lines (Catris!): 1200 × Level

Bonus points are awarded for soft and hard drops.

🚀 Why Catris?

Catris combines classic puzzle gameplay with:

Modern Android performance
Clean UI/UX design
Immersive sound & vibration systems
Smooth progression and challenge scaling

It’s simple to play, but hard to master.

🔒 Privacy First
No internet required
No data collection
No ads or tracking
All progress stored locally on your device
